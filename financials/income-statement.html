Assumptions + notes
  1. NGF has distributed $5K+ in AI credits to date (nextgenerationfounders.org). We project $12K with Lovable plus one or two new partners (prospects: Replit, Canva, StreetCode). In-kind credits appear as both revenue and program expense, so they do not change net assets.
  2. Grants are targets, not commitments: e.g. university public-service and entrepreneurship funds and local community foundations.
  3. Sponsorship assumes one AI partner contributes cash toward food and travel.
  4. Assumes 24 workshops (about two per month) at ~25 students each, ~600 students in total.
  5. All facilitators are unpaid student volunteers. Volunteer time is not recorded as revenue or expense under nonprofit accounting rules (ASC 958) unless it requires specialized skills.
  6. NGF has been a 501(c)(3) since January 2026 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer). All figures are team projections for this class, not NGF financial statements.

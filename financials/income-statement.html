Notes and Assumptions

  1. In-kind AI tool credits are recorded at fair value as both contribution revenue and program expense; they do not affect the change in net assets. NGF has distributed over $5,000 in credits to date; the projection assumes $12,000 from its current technology partner (Lovable) and one to two additional partners.
  2. Grants are projected targets from university and local foundation sources; none are committed as of this date.
  3. Assumes one technology partner contributes cash sponsorship toward workshop costs.
  4. Assumes 24 workshops (approximately two per month) serving about 25 students each, or 600 students in total; $150 per workshop for supplies and food and $60 per workshop for travel.
  5. Includes insurance, state and federal filings, accounting, and website costs.
  6. Workshops are delivered by unpaid student volunteers. Contributed services are not recognized because they do not require specialized skills (ASC 958-605).
  7. Program services represent 86.3% of total expenses. Cash cost per student, excluding in-kind credits, is approximately $14 ($8,440 ÷ 600); total cost per student is approximately $34 ($20,440 ÷ 600).
